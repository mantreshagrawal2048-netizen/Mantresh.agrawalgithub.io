How I trade

I execute short-term trades with disciplined attention to market trends and data. My analysis starts with price structure, where liquidity sits and when the market is active, using a higher timeframe (1H) to set direction and lower timeframes (5 to 15 minutes) to time entries.

I wrote down my approach to gold in The XAUUSD Playbook, linked in the projects section below.

Backtest results, not live trading

Systematic strategy research

I also design rule-based strategies and test them before trusting them. Two Bank Nifty options-buying strategies were backtested on Tradetron with 5-minute data:

  • KuberSharpe 1.75, max drawdown -33.1%
  • GriffinSharpe 2.06, max drawdown -37.2%

Checking the trade logs showed that most of the profit came from a few trades and that the edge was not statistically significant, so I treat both as research-stage.

Live IPO GMP Tracker

Web app built with Python, GitHub Actions and JavaScript, October 2026

Every website quotes a different grey market premium (GMP) for the same IPO. This tool collects GMP from five public sources and shows one consensus estimate and expected listing return for each IPO, then back-tests how well GMP has predicted actual listing gains. It runs at zero hosting cost.
